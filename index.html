The journey of a harvest

From the soil to your table, MarketLink connects growers directly with buyers in one seamless process.

1. Plan & Publish

Farmers list their upcoming crops, setting clear harvest dates and fair farm-gate prices before they even start cutting.

2. Reserve in Advance

Households and kitchens browse local farms, reserving their exact needs. The farmer secures a guaranteed sale.

3. Same-Day Harvest

Crops stay in the ground until the day of pickup. Farmers harvest exactly what was ordered—meaning zero food waste.

4. Collect & Enjoy

Buyers collect their fresh produce at designated neighborhood hubs, paying a fraction of retail prices while farmers earn more.

What changes when
you cut the chain

Food typically passes through three to five intermediaries before your plate. Each one adds cost, time, and spoilage. Here's the difference when you don't.

Freshness you can taste

Regular market vegetables spend days in hot transit lorries. MarketLink produce is harvested on the day of your pickup. That's flavour and nutrition a cold chain can't match.

Honest pricing both ways

Middlemen absorb 40 to 60 percent of produce margins. Farmers earn more, buyers pay less, and neither party subsidises a distribution chain they never asked for.

Harvest only what sells

Pre-orders let growers harvest to demand. Every crate has a confirmed buyer, so nothing rots at the roadside waiting for a truck that might not come.
